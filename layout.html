The Care Guide · For new + seasoned keepers

The Keeper’s Grimoire*Crested Gecko Care Guide

Five rites, seven days, six omens, and the instruments to keep a crested gecko for fifteen to twenty years.

The Rites✦The Week✦Omens✦Instruments

* This Grimoire is meant to be used as a starting point for your crestie’s care.

You might find that your own animal has preferences that dip outside of standard practice, and that’s okay.

Please don’t hesitate to contact me with any questions. ♡

The Five RitesRite I of V · Air

The VesselCrested Gecko Enclosure Setup

Give it room to rise

Crested geckos are arboreal. They live up high, so build tall rather than wide. Fill the top half with branches, cork and dense leaves for cover, and use a vent, mesh, or multiple holes in the enclosure for proper airflow while still retaining humidity.

Adult
18×18×24 in
Juvenile
12×12×18 in

{{ lm_gstar }}Do

Vertical enclosure, lots of climbing branches and foliage, good airflow.

✕Forbidden

Tanks wider than they are tall, bare walls, sealed glass with no ventilation.

“I give my vessel room to rise.”

The Five RitesRite II of V · Fire

The HearthCrested Gecko Temperature

Hold the warmth, banish the heat

Normal room temperature is usually perfect. Heat lamps are NOT needed. Heat is the real danger: above 82°F they get stressed, and 85°F or more can kill. In winter a gentle drop at night is fine.

Day
72–78°F
Night
65–72°F
Never above
82°F

{{ lm_gstar }}Do

A thermometer at the top and bottom of the enclosure. Keep it out of direct sun.

✕Forbidden

Heat lamps in a warm room, or an enclosure by a window or radiator.

“I hold the warmth steady and the heat at bay.”

The Five RitesRite III of V · Water

The MistCrested Gecko Humidity & Misting

Mist at dusk, dry by dawn

Offer water two ways. Mist the enclosure at dusk so your gecko can lap droplets off the leaves, and keep a shallow dish of fresh water out at all times. Let the enclosure dry out between mistings to prevent mold and skin trouble.

Baseline
50–60%
After mist
70–80%

{{ lm_gstar }}Do

Use a hygrometer. Aim for a spike after misting, then a dry-down.

✕Forbidden

Constantly wet, or bone-dry for days.

“I nourish with mist and let the dawn dry it.”

The Five RitesRite IV of V · Earth

The FeastCrested Gecko Diet & Feeding

Feed the body well

A complete powdered gecko diet mixed with water covers everything they need. Gut-loaded insects dusted with calcium are helpful for adolescent growth, but some adults still enjoy them as well. Remove old food every 24–48 hours so it doesn’t spoil.

Diet
Complete powder
Insects
Optional, 1×/wk

{{ lm_gstar }}Do

Complete diet 2–3× a week, insects 1× a week.

✕Forbidden

Fruit-only diets and wild-caught insects.

“I feed my vessel well, and I am fed in turn.”

The Five RitesRite V of V · Aether

The BondCrested Gecko Handling & Settling In

Earn the trust

Give a new gecko two weeks to settle in before you handle it. Keep sessions short and low to the ground, and let it walk from one hand to the other. Never grab the tail: it can drop off, and it won’t grow back.

Settle-in
2 weeks
Session
5–15 min

{{ lm_gstar }}Do

Sit on the floor, hand-walk for 5–15 minutes, keep a calm presence.

✕Forbidden

Grabbing, chasing, or handling during shed or right after a meal.

“Patience is my offering; trust is my reward.”
